Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

BUILD (“we”, “us”, “our”) is operated by an individual seller based in Romania. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website or purchase BUILD, and how it is used. If you have questions, contact us at buildphilosophy26@gmail.com.

What this website itself collects

This website is a static site with no account system, no newsletter sign-up, and no analytics or advertising trackers of its own. Browsing this site does not set any first-party cookies.

Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records standard server logs for every visit — such as IP address, browser type, and timestamp — for security and reliability purposes. This is standard practice for any website and is processed by Vercel as our hosting infrastructure provider.

What happens when you make a purchase

Purchases of BUILD (the book) and BUILD Community are processed entirely by Whop.com, our order processor and Merchant of Record. Purchase buttons on this site link directly to Whop, where Whop's checkout collects the information needed to complete your order — including your email address, billing details, and payment information.

We do not see or store your full payment details ourselves. Whop acts as the seller of record for these transactions and is independently responsible for that data under its own privacy policy: whop.com/privacy →.

This website loads no third-party checkout scripts of its own — purchase buttons simply link to Whop's website, where Whop may set cookies necessary to process your order.

What we personally receive and use

As the seller, we receive order notifications from Whop (such as your name and email) so we can deliver what you purchased — for BUILD Community, this currently means manually reaching out to you by email to arrange access, since it's a new, small-scale offering without automated onboarding yet. We use this information only to fulfill your order and respond to related questions, and do not sell or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your rights

If you are located in the European Union or the UK, you have rights under the GDPR regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of data we hold about you. Since most of your purchase data is held by Whop as the Merchant of Record, requests about payment records may need to be directed to them directly. For anything we personally hold (such as order-fulfillment emails), contact us at buildphilosophy26@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or our offerings change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to buildphilosophy26@gmail.com.
