The idea

BUILD is not about finding a secret.

It is about learning to see things differently.

Most people are taught what to do. Very few are taught how to see.

BUILD brings together practical ideas about value, uncertainty, markets, offers, marketing, systems, leadership and long-term thinking — organized into one coherent way of approaching decisions.

It is designed to be read, questioned and applied — not simply finished.

A first look

Ideas worth building on.

Six statements from inside BUILD — not conclusions to memorize, but ideas to keep testing against your own decisions.

01

Conviction is not evidence.

Every business plan is a stack of assumptions wearing the costume of a plan. The builder's habit is to turn "I think" into "I tested" as often, and as cheaply, as possible.

The goal isn't certainty. It's evidence.

02

Value before profit.

The businesses that last obsess over the value they create and treat profit as the natural, if delayed, result. When short-term revenue and long-term trust point in opposite directions, choose trust.

Because trust compounds.

03

Think in systems.

A business is not a collection of tasks you personally perform. It is a network of systems. When something goes wrong, don't only ask "how do I fix this?" Ask:

"What part of the system allowed this to happen?"

04

Customers buy outcomes.

People rarely buy a product simply because of what it is. They buy what they believe it will do for them.

A feature is evidence. The outcome is the reason.

05

Price is a signal.

Price isn't only a number. It communicates something about the value, positioning and confidence behind what you're offering.

Price is part of the message.

06

Obvious doesn't mean easy.

Some of the most useful ideas in BUILD may sound obvious. Knowing something and consistently practicing it are two very different things.

Sometimes the work is applying what you already understand.

From the author

BUILD didn't start as a book.

It started as lessons that were expensive to learn. Over time, the same patterns kept appearing: assumptions that hadn't been tested, offers whose value wasn't clear, businesses built around the founder instead of a system, and decisions made from conviction when evidence was available.

BUILD is an attempt to put those patterns in one place — not as a collection of secrets, but as a set of ideas worth returning to.

"I'll admit something: the first real product I ever built, I skipped this chapter entirely."

Let's be direct

  • This isn't a promise of overnight success.
  • It isn't a formula.
  • It isn't a collection of hacks.
  • It isn't designed to make you feel like you know more.
  • It's designed to make you think better about what you're building.

Who it's for

You don't need to be a founder.

BUILD is for anyone who wants to understand how better decisions are made, how value is created, how opportunities are evaluated — and how ideas become something real.

Building a business

You already have something moving, and want to understand the decisions behind its next stage.

Thinking about starting

You have an idea, but don't want to spend months building before finding out whether it deserves to exist.

Working inside a business

You want to understand the systems, incentives and decisions behind the work you already do.

Building something else

A career. A project. A skill. A life. The way of thinking still applies.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Is BUILD a course?
No. The book itself is a written body of knowledge — no video lessons or required schedule. BUILD Community and BUILD 1:1 add a private community and optional personal guidance for people who want to go further, but the book stays exactly what it is: something you read at your own pace.
What's the difference between BUILD, Community and 1:1?
BUILD is the book — the knowledge, once, yours to keep. BUILD Community adds a private space to discuss it with people doing the same work. BUILD 1:1 adds personal guidance on top of that, including one 1:1 session a month. Each level includes everything in the one before it.
Can I start with the book and upgrade later?
Yes. The book stands on its own. Move to Community or 1:1 whenever it makes sense for you — there's no required path or timeline.
Do I need to be an entrepreneur?
No. The principles are built around decision-making, value, markets and systems — concepts that apply well beyond entrepreneurship.
Is this a get-rich-quick book?
No. BUILD does not promise a specific income, business model or shortcut. It focuses on the thinking and systems behind better decisions.
Is BUILD just an ebook?
The core product is a digital book, but it also contains case studies, frameworks, key takeaways and 24 practical workbooks.
How do I use it?
Read it straight through, work through one chapter per week, or return to the chapter that matches the problem you're facing.
